Check the roofs

NZ rooftop solar map ·

The map draws every roof from laser scans and aerial photos. It often gets parts wrong. You can help by checking the drawings against the photo, one question at a time.

  • Each card asks about one thing: the roof's outline, its roof lines, its vents and skylights, or its solar panels.
  • Look only at that one thing and say Right, Wrong or Can't tell. Hold Photo only to see the roof without the drawing.
  • If you notice anything else wrong, tap Something else is wrong and say what. That matters most.
  • Several people check every roof, and some roofs have known answers, so don't worry about the odd mistake.

No sign-in. We keep a random number for this browser, your answers and anything you type. Please don't type names or contact details.
