Wholesale price history

Average wholesale power price at the grid, by time of day, in today's dollars.

The spot price at this grid node for every half-hour since October 1996 (Electricity Authority final prices): what retailers pay for power delivered there, and what a generator feeding in there is paid. Not what homes pay: bills add lines, metering, levies, the retailer's margin and GST.

Each average weights every half-hour equally, not by how much a solar panel makes in it. Sunlight hours: the sun above a flat horizon at that town, not allowing for mountains or cloud; a half-hour counts when its middle is lit. Peak: 7–10am and 6–9pm, every day, the hours the bill model prices as peak. Overnight: 11pm–7am.

Today's dollars: each half-hour's price scaled by Stats NZ's consumers price index (all groups) to the latest quarter published; days after that quarter are left as paid. Dollars of the day: the price as paid.
